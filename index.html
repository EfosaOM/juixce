About

A creative anchor for artists finding their sound.

At the core, Juice is a music producer who often wears many hats — creative direction and songwriting through final production, sound design, and live performance arrangements. The work starts in Lagos, where keys and click tracks for local artists became a full-time craft with records now streaming in the millions, then widened on U.S. tours as supporting creative director.
